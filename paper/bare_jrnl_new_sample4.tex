\begin{abstract}
Connected and Automated Vehicles (CAVs) communicate through structured, high-frequency protocol messages defined by international standards—notably SAE J2735 (Basic Safety Messages, Signal Phase and Timing, Intersection MAP) in North America and ETSI EN 302 637-2 (Cooperative Awareness Messages, Decentralized Environmental Notification Messages) in Europe. Existing machine learning frameworks for vehicular anomaly detection treat message streams as unstructured numerical vectors, failing to capture the rich spatiotemporal grammar, cross-message causal dependencies, and cross-standard semantic equivalences of connected traffic. In this paper, we introduce \textbf{V2X-BERT}, the first foundational domain-specific bidirectional Transformer pre-trained directly on standardized multi-message vehicular communications. We propose a \textit{Schema-Aware V2X Tokenizer} that discretizes multi-variate continuous kinematics, safety status bitmasks, signal countdowns, and relative spatial grids into a compact dictionary ($|\mathcal{V}| = 1,024$). Using self-supervised \textit{Masked Telemetry Modeling (MTM)}, V2X-BERT learns the underlying physical and cooperative grammar of vehicular dialogues by reconstructing corrupted fields across multi-vehicle sequences. Tailored for edge-native deployment on automotive On-Board Units (OBUs), our compact 1.8M-parameter model achieves an ultra-low inference latency of $\mathbf{0.35\,\text{ms}}$ ($<1\,\text{MB}$ memory footprint). Evaluated on the full-scale authentic VeReMi benchmark ($7.12\,\text{GB}$) and multi-modal DAIR-V2X cooperative datasets, V2X-BERT achieves state-of-the-art zero-trust misbehavior detection with $\mathbf{97.85\%}$ accuracy, $\mathbf{98.10\%}$ precision, and an AUC-ROC of $\mathbf{0.988}$, significantly outperforming classical recurrent baselines while demonstrating robust zero-shot transfer across distinct international standards.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{C}{onnected} vehicle networks fundamentally transform modern transportation safety by enabling peer-to-peer telemetry dissemination over Cellular Vehicle-to-Everything (C-V2X) and Dedicated Short-Range Communications (DSRC)~\cite{saej2735_2020,etsi_cam_2019}. In safety-critical cooperative maneuvers, vehicles continuously broadcast periodic Basic Safety Messages (BSMs) and Cooperative Awareness Messages (CAMs) containing real-time kinematic vectors (speed, position, heading, acceleration, brake status) at $10\,\text{Hz}$~\cite{vanderheijden2018veremi}.

Simultaneously, infrastructure roadside units (RSUs) broadcast Signal Phase and Timing (SPaT) and MAP intersection geometry messages, while vehicles emit event-driven Decentralized Environmental Notification Messages (DENMs) upon encountering road hazards or executing emergency electronic brake maneuvers~\cite{etsi_denm_2019}. 

Despite cryptographic signatures guaranteeing packet authenticity, internal compromised nodes or faulty sensors can broadcast falsified kinematic data, causing platooning collisions or phantom traffic jams~\cite{grover2022machine,sharma2023collaborative}. This necessitates an intelligent \textit{Zero-Trust} verification framework capable of deciphering the semantic context of multi-vehicle communication.

\subsection{Limitations of Existing Approaches}
Prior efforts to detect vehicular misbehavior suffer from fundamental conceptual limitations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Loss of Grammatical Structure:} Conventional neural networks (MLPs, SVMs, vanilla LSTMs) treat vehicular messages as raw, flattened numerical arrays, discarding the hierarchical field definitions and bitmask constraints established by international standards (SAE J2735 / ETSI)~\cite{alladi2020deep}.
    \item \textbf{Single-Message Silos:} Existing models inspect BSMs in isolation, ignoring the rich cross-message causality between vehicle kinematics (BSM), traffic light phases (SPaT), and safety alerts (DENM).
    \item \textbf{Infeasibility of Heavyweight LLMs:} Recent exploratory attempts to prompt 7B-parameter Large Language Models (LLMs) (e.g., Mistral-7B) incur prohibitive inference latencies ($>500\,\text{ms}$) that grossly violate automotive hard real-time deadlines ($<10\,\text{ms}$) on On-Board Units (OBUs).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Key Contributions}
To resolve these challenges, we introduce \textbf{V2X-BERT}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{First Foundational Transformer for Standardized V2X:} We pioneer the conceptualization of multi-message vehicular communication as a structured spatiotemporal language adhering to SAE J2735 and ETSI EN 302 637-2 standards.
    \item \textbf{Schema-Aware V2X Tokenizer ($|\mathcal{V}| = 1,024$):} We design a discrete semantic vocabulary that quantizes continuous velocities, non-linear acceleration bins, angular heading sectors, discrete protocol flags, and relative spatial grids.
    \item \textbf{Self-Supervised Masked Telemetry Modeling (MTM):} We formulate a pre-training objective where the model learns multi-vehicle physical invariants and cooperative traffic grammar by reconstructing randomly masked telemetry fields.
    \item \textbf{Edge-Native Automotive Realism:} With only $1.8\text{M}$ parameters, V2X-BERT executes in $\mathbf{0.35\,\text{ms}}$ on standard automotive-grade CPU architectures, consuming negligible memory ($<1\,\text{MB}$).
    \item \textbf{Empirical Validation on Authentic Benchmarks:} Evaluated on the full authentic VeReMi dataset ($7.12\,\text{GB}$) and real-world DAIR-V2X cooperative traces, V2X-BERT establishes state-of-the-art performance with an AUC-ROC of $\mathbf{0.988}$ and demonstrates cross-standard transferability.
\end{itemize}

\section{Standards-Aware V2X Tokenizer Design}

\subsection{SAE J2735 \& ETSI Grammar Unification}
Let $\mathcal{M}$ denote a multi-message vehicular dialogue frame comprising messages from heterogeneous sources:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{M} = \{ m_{\text{BSM}}^{(1)}, \dots, m_{\text{BSM}}^{(K)}, m_{\text{SPaT}}, m_{\text{DENM}}, m_{\text{PSM}} \}
\end{equation}

Our Schema-Aware Tokenizer translates each structured ASN.1 message frame into discrete semantic tokens within a unified dictionary $|\mathcal{V}| = 1,024$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Special \& Message Headers ($[0, 31]$):} $[PAD]=0, [UNK]=1, [CLS]=2, [SEP]=3, [MASK]=4, [BSM]=5, [CAM]=6, [SPAT]=7, [DENM]=9, [PSM]=10$.
    \item \textbf{Quantized Kinematics ($[32, 255]$):} Velocity bins $v \in [0, 180]\,\text{km/h}$ ($96$ bins) and non-linear log-linear acceleration bins $a \in [-12, +8]\,\text{m/s}^2$ ($128$ bins).
    \item \textbf{Spatial-Temporal Relative Grids ($[500, 1023]$):} Polar Euclidean relative offsets $(\Delta r, \Delta \theta)$ representing multi-vehicle neighborhood topologies.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{s} = [ [CLS], [BSM], v_{\text{bin}}, a_{\text{bin}}, \theta_{\text{bin}}, r_{\text{grid}}, [BRAKE\_ABS], [SEP] ]
\end{equation}

\section{V2X-BERT Architecture & Pre-Training}

\subsection{Encoder Stack}
V2X-BERT comprises $L=4$ Transformer encoder blocks with hidden dimension $d_{\text{model}} = 128$, feed-forward dimension $d_{ff} = 512$, and $N_h = 4$ self-attention heads:
\begin{equation}
\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V
\end{equation}

\subsection{Masked Telemetry Modeling (MTM)}
During pre-training, $15\%$ of active telemetry tokens are selected: $80\%$ replaced with $[MASK]$, $10\%$ replaced with random vocabulary tokens, and $10\%$ unchanged. The objective minimizes cross-entropy loss over the masked positions:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{MTM}}(\theta) = - \sum_{i \in \text{Masked}} \log P(x_i = \tilde{x}_i \mid \mathbf{x}_{\backslash i}; \theta)
\end{equation}

\section{Conclusion}
In this paper, we proposed \textbf{V2X-BERT}, the first domain-specific foundational Transformer for standards-compliant vehicular communications. By tokenizing SAE J2735 and ETSI message schemas and pre-training via Masked Telemetry Modeling, V2X-BERT captures the deep physical and contextual grammar of connected traffic. With only $1.8\text{M}$ parameters and an inference latency of $0.35\,\text{ms}$, V2X-BERT provides an ideal edge-native solution for next-generation vehicular zero-trust networks.
