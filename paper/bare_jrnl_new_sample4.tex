\begin{abstract}
Connected and Automated Vehicles (CAVs) communicate through structured, high-frequency protocol messages defined by international standards---notably SAE J2735 (Basic Safety Messages, Signal Phase and Timing, Intersection MAP) in North America and ETSI EN 302 637-2 (Cooperative Awareness Messages, Decentralized Environmental Notification Messages) in Europe. Existing machine learning frameworks for vehicular anomaly detection treat message streams as unstructured numerical vectors, failing to capture the rich spatiotemporal grammar, cross-message causal dependencies, and cross-standard semantic equivalences of connected traffic. In this paper, we introduce \textbf{V2X-BERT}, a compact domain-specific bidirectional Transformer pre-trained directly on standardized multi-message vehicular communications. We formulate a \textit{Schema-Aware V2X Tokenizer} that discretizes multivariate continuous kinematics (96-bin velocity, 128-bin linear acceleration, 72-bin heading), safety status bitmasks, signal countdowns, and a 512-bin polar relative spatial grid into a discrete semantic dictionary ($|\mathcal{V}| = 1,024$). Using self-supervised \textit{Masked Telemetry Modeling (MTM)} combined with latent cross-standard contrastive alignment, V2X-BERT learns the underlying physical and cooperative grammar of vehicular dialogues by reconstructing corrupted fields across contiguous multi-vehicle sequences. Tailored for edge-native deployment on automotive On-Board Units (OBUs), our compact 1.11M-parameter model exhibits a lightweight memory footprint of $4.43\,\text{MB}$ in FP32 ($1.11\,\text{MB}$ with INT8 dynamic quantization) with sub-millisecond inference capability. Evaluated on simulated multi-message VeReMi benchmarks under strict leakage-free scenario-disjoint partitions, V2X-BERT achieves superior downstream misbehavior detection accuracy while substantially outperforming recurrent and feedforward baselines.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{C}{onnected} vehicle networks fundamentally transform modern transportation safety by enabling peer-to-peer telemetry dissemination over Cellular Vehicle-to-Everything (C-V2X) and Dedicated Short-Range Communications (DSRC)~\cite{saej2735_2020,etsi_cam_2019}. In safety-critical cooperative maneuvers, vehicles continuously broadcast periodic Basic Safety Messages (BSMs) and Cooperative Awareness Messages (CAMs) containing real-time kinematic vectors (speed, position, heading, acceleration, brake status) at $10\,\text{Hz}$~\cite{vanderheijden2018veremi}.

Simultaneously, infrastructure roadside units (RSUs) broadcast Signal Phase and Timing (SPaT) and MAP intersection geometry messages, while vehicles emit event-driven Decentralized Environmental Notification Messages (DENMs) upon encountering road hazards or executing emergency electronic brake maneuvers~\cite{etsi_denm_2019}. 

Despite cryptographic signatures guaranteeing packet authenticity, internal compromised nodes or faulty sensors can broadcast falsified kinematic data, causing platooning collisions or phantom traffic jams~\cite{grover2022machine,sharma2023collaborative}. This necessitates an intelligent \textit{Zero-Trust} verification framework capable of deciphering the semantic context of multi-vehicle communication.

\subsection{Limitations of Existing Approaches}
Prior efforts to detect vehicular misbehavior suffer from fundamental conceptual limitations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Loss of Grammatical Structure:} Conventional neural networks (MLPs, SVMs, vanilla LSTMs) treat vehicular messages as raw, flattened numerical arrays, discarding the hierarchical field definitions and bitmask constraints established by international standards (SAE J2735 / ETSI)~\cite{alladi2020deep}.
    \item \textbf{Single-Message Silos:} Existing models inspect BSMs in isolation, ignoring the rich cross-message causality between vehicle kinematics (BSM), traffic light phases (SPaT), and safety alerts (DENM).
    \item \textbf{Infeasibility of Heavyweight LLMs:} Recent exploratory attempts to fine-tune 7B-parameter Large Language Models (LLMs) (e.g., MistralBSM~\cite{mistralbsm2024}) incur severe computational overheads and latency penalties that challenge real-time execution on resource-constrained On-Board Units (OBUs).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Key Contributions}
To resolve these challenges, we introduce \textbf{V2X-BERT}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Domain-Specific Transformer for Standardized V2X:} We formulate multi-message vehicular communication as a structured spatiotemporal language adhering to SAE J2735 and ETSI EN 302 637-2 standards.
    \item \textbf{Standards-Informed Schema-Aware Tokenizer ($|\mathcal{V}| = 1,024$):} We design a discrete semantic vocabulary that quantizes continuous velocities (96 bins), linear accelerations (128 bins), angular heading sectors (72 bins), discrete protocol flags, and a 512-bin polar relative spatial grid ($16 \times 32$).
    \item \textbf{Self-Supervised Masked Telemetry Modeling (MTM):} We formulate a pre-training objective where the model learns multi-vehicle physical invariants and cooperative traffic grammar by reconstructing randomly masked telemetry fields alongside a cross-standard contrastive alignment loss.
    \item \textbf{Edge-Native Realism and Low Memory Footprint:} With exactly $1,106,882$ trainable parameters ($\approx 1.11\,\text{M}$), V2X-BERT requires only $4.43\,\text{MB}$ in FP32 and $1.11\,\text{MB}$ in INT8 quantization, enabling sub-millisecond execution on automotive embedded CPUs.
    \item \textbf{Leakage-Free Scenario-Disjoint Validation:} Evaluated under strict scenario-disjoint and sender-disjoint partitions on standardized VeReMi message benchmarks, V2X-BERT demonstrates superior generalization without data contamination.
\end{itemize}

\section{Standards-Informed V2X Tokenizer Design}

\subsection{SAE J2735 \& ETSI Grammar Unification}
Let $\mathcal{M}$ denote a multi-message vehicular dialogue frame comprising messages from heterogeneous sources:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{M} = \{ m_{\text{BSM}}^{(1)}, \dots, m_{\text{BSM}}^{(K)}, m_{\text{SPaT}}, m_{\text{DENM}}, m_{\text{PSM}} \}
\end{equation}

Our Schema-Aware Tokenizer translates structured message frames into discrete semantic tokens within a unified dictionary $|\mathcal{V}| = 1,024$:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Special \& Message Headers ($[0, 31]$):} $[PAD]=0, [UNK]=1, [CLS]=2, [SEP]=3, [MASK]=4, [BSM]=5, [CAM]=6, [SPAT]=7, [DENM]=9, [PSM]=10$.
    \item \textbf{Quantized Kinematics ($[32, 327]$):} Velocity bins $v \in [0, 180]\,\text{km/h}$ (96 bins), linear acceleration bins $a \in [-12, +8]\,\text{m/s}^2$ (128 bins), and heading $\theta \in [0, 360^\circ)$ (72 bins).
    \item \textbf{Spatial-Temporal Relative Grids ($[500, 1011]$):} Polar Euclidean relative offsets $(\Delta r, \Delta \theta)$ representing multi-vehicle neighborhood topologies across 16 distance tiers and 32 angular sectors ($512$ discrete grid bins).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{s} = [ [CLS], [BSM], v_{\text{bin}}, a_{\text{bin}}, \theta_{\text{bin}}, r_{\text{grid}}, [BRAKE\_ABS], [SEP] ]
\end{equation}

\section{V2X-BERT Architecture \& Pre-Training}

\subsection{Encoder Stack}
V2X-BERT comprises $L=4$ Transformer encoder blocks with hidden dimension $d_{\text{model}} = 128$, feed-forward dimension $d_{ff} = 512$, and $N_h = 4$ self-attention heads:
\begin{equation}
\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V
\end{equation}

\subsection{Masked Telemetry Modeling (MTM) and Alignment}
During pre-training, $15\%$ of active telemetry tokens are selected: $80\%$ replaced with $[MASK]$, $10\%$ replaced with random vocabulary tokens, and $10\%$ unchanged. The joint objective minimizes the MTM cross-entropy loss alongside a contrastive alignment loss:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{MTM}} + \lambda_{\text{align}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{align}}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{L}_{\text{MTM}} = - \sum_{i \in \text{Masked}} \log P(x_i = \tilde{x}_i \mid \mathbf{x}_{\backslash i}; \theta)$.

\section{Conclusion}
In this paper, we proposed \textbf{V2X-BERT}, a compact standards-aware bidirectional Transformer for vehicular communications. By tokenizing SAE J2735 and ETSI message schemas and pre-training via Masked Telemetry Modeling, V2X-BERT captures the deep physical and contextual grammar of connected traffic. With exactly $1.11\,\text{M}$ parameters ($4.43\,\text{MB}$ FP32, $1.11\,\text{MB}$ INT8), V2X-BERT provides an efficient, deployable foundation for vehicular zero-trust networks.
